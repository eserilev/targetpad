# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First version: hold a button and a gold arrow marks your target, step through them with the d-pad or a stick, let go to keep the target.
- Settings: the hold button (also in the game's Key Bindings) and what steps (d-pad, right stick or left stick).
- /tp test checks it in the game; /tp shots takes a screenshot of each target you step to.
